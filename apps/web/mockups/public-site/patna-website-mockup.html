Our story

Africa must shape its own climate future

PATNA was born from a simple but urgent conviction: Africa must shape its future in global climate and energy decision-making. For too long, international policies affecting African economies, infrastructure, and livelihoods have been designed with limited African participation.

What began as a focused effort on maritime decarbonisation has evolved into a broader platform supporting energy transitions, climate governance, and institutional readiness across sectors.

A community built on African expertise

The PATNA Community brings together academics, policymakers, industry leaders, and civil society practitioners to collaborate on Africa's most pressing climate and energy challenges.

Who's in PATNA

Four expert cohorts, one mission

PATNA is structured around four professional cohorts, each bringing distinct expertise. Members may belong to multiple cohorts, but identify one as primary. Cohort leads guide engagement, moderate discussions, and provide strategic direction.

"Membership is structured, intentional, and governed — not a mailing list."
